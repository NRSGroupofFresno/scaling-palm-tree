About Us

Our Mission

The Legal Advocacy Platform is committed to providing free legal assistance and authorized representative services to low-income families in need. We believe that everyone deserves access to justice, regardless of their financial situation.

What We Do

We provide comprehensive legal advocacy services including:

  • Legal representation and advocacy
  • Self-help resources for court documents
  • Authorized representative services
  • Document preparation assistance
  • Legal consultation and guidance
  • Case management support

NRS Group of Fresno

This platform is operated by NRS Group of Fresno, a trusted provider of legal advocacy services. We are the parent company of several community-focused initiatives dedicated to helping families navigate the legal system.

Who We Serve

We primarily serve low-income families who meet our income eligibility guidelines. Our services are completely free for qualifying individuals and families.

Areas of Practice

Family Law

Divorce, custody, child support, and domestic relations matters

Housing

Eviction defense, landlord-tenant disputes, and housing rights

Immigration

Immigration applications, naturalization, and asylum cases

Employment

Wage disputes, workplace discrimination, and employment rights

Consumer Rights

Consumer protection, debt defense, and credit issues

Small Claims

Small claims court assistance and representation
