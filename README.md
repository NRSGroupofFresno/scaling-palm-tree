# scaling-palm-tree

Legal Advocacy & Authorized Rep Services and Self Help for Court Documents Free If Low-Income Families Plus Parent Company of other projects

## About

NRS Group of Fresno provides legal advocacy and authorized representative services, with a focus on:
- Free self-help services for court documents
- Support for low-income families
- Legal advocacy and representation
- Parent company for related projects

## Mission

Our mission is to provide accessible legal services and support to low-income families who need assistance with court documents and legal representation.

## Features

- Legal advocacy services
- Authorized representative services
- Free self-help for court documents
- Support for low-income families
- Parent organization for related legal service projects
